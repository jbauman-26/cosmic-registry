# Editing standard for anything a kit writes

Apply this to every sentence shown to a person: audit notes, summaries, and any email copy a user asks for.

## Hard rules
- No em or en dashes.
- No invented facts, quotes, numbers or results. Show a gap as [NEEDS NUMBER] or [NEEDS SOURCE].
- Use plain "is" and "are". Avoid "serves as", "stands as", "represents".
- Cut "not just X, but Y" and "it's not X, it's Y".
- Cut throat-clearing such as "it's important to note", "ultimately", "in conclusion".
- Cut filler words such as delve, robust, seamless, leverage, unlock, transformative, holistic.

## Score before returning
Score each from 0 to 2. Pass is 16 of 20. Below that, rewrite.
1. No em dashes.
2. No filler words from the list above.
3. Plain verbs instead of elevated ones.
4. No "not X, but Y" scaffolds.
5. No throat-clearing.
6. Sentence length varies, with short and long lines near each other.
7. Specific names, numbers and places.
8. A clear point of view.
9. Lists only where the content is a list.
10. Opens on the point and ends on something useful.

## Do not fake a human voice
Skip "honestly", "here's the thing", staged self-correction and random casual words.

## Required step
Have a separate editing pass check the writing against this page before it reaches the user. If your agent cannot run a separate pass, re-read the draft against this page as a second step.
