# Cash cycle and weeks of cover

Works out how many days cash is tied up between paying suppliers and getting paid by customers, and whether the stock on hand covers the time a new order takes to arrive. It uses the store's own cost of goods and four figures you give it: stock value at cost, supplier payment terms, supplier lead time and payout timing.

- Reads: Shopify orders and product costs, and the figures you type in.
- Writes: nothing. It sends nothing either.
- Costs: nothing from a provider.
- You approve: nothing, because nothing changes.
