# Channel claim audit

Once a month, sets what each ad platform says its ads earned beside what Shopify recorded, and beside the platform's own past. Four plain checks mark each platform pass, watch or fail. A platform that fails twice is flagged: its numbers should not steer budget until a holdout test settles it.

- Reads: Shopify orders, Meta's spend and claims, and Google Ads when connected.
- Writes: nothing. It sends nothing either.
- Costs: nothing from a provider.
- You approve: nothing, because nothing changes.
