# Benchmarks

Starting points, not laws. Replace any of them with the owner's own targets when the owner has them, and say which ones you used.

## Layer 1: decides scale, hold or cut

| Metric | How it is worked out | Starting target |
|---|---|---|
| Front-end cash ROAS | Cash collected in 30 days / ad spend | 3x to 4x when starting, 2.5x to 3x at scale. Under 2x is a cash flow problem |
| Contracted ROAS | Total contracted value / ad spend | Owner's target |
| Cost per acquisition | Ad spend / new customers | Owner's target |
| Contribution margin | Cash collected - ad spend - commissions - direct delivery cost | 1.0x of spend or better before any scaling step |

## Layer 2: finds the broken stage

| Stage | Metric | Starting target |
|---|---|---|
| Generate leads | Cost per lead | $30 to $50 for high-ticket lead gen. $0-30 great, $31-50 good, $51-80 poor, $81+ bad |
| Leads book | Booked calls / leads | 60% or more |
| Leads book | Cost per booked call | $120 to $200 |
| Bookings show | Shows / booked calls | 65% minimum, 70% to 80% goal |
| Shows close | Closes / qualified shows | 20% to 25% floor. Below that is a sales problem, not an ads one |
| Shows close | Upfront cash / contracted value | 40% or more |

## Layer 3: finds the exact leak

| Metric | Starting target |
|---|---|
| CTR (all) | 2.5%+ ready to scale, 1.5% to 2.49% solid, under 1% being ignored |
| Link CTR | Above 2% is good |
| CPM | Under $10 scale, $10 to $20 normal, $20 to $40 high, $40+ fix now |
| Frequency | Under 2 underexposed, 2 to 3 safe, 3 to 5 fatigue likely |
| Disqualified calls | Over 35% means add friction to the form. Over 50% means lead quality is the constraint |
| No-shows | Under 20% good, 30% acceptable, over 35% not acceptable |

For a store with no calls, the stages collapse to: traffic (CPM, CTR), conversion (site conversion rate, order value), and payback (Shopify net revenue / Meta spend). Use the `meta-spend-check` kit for that side.
