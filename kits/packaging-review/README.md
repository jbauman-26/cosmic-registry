# Packaging review

Before you publish a title, thumbnail idea, hook, script or post, your agent sends it to Cosmic Cloud and shows you what comes back: a verdict, a score out of 100, each problem with a fix in plain words, and rewrites you can use. When your workspace has checked numbers, the review can use them as proof, and it never uses a number the checks held back. The judging happens on Cosmic Cloud, so the kit itself holds no rules.

- Needs: a Pro or Agency workspace on Cosmic Cloud. On the free plan the call answers that it is part of Pro.
- Reads: only the draft you send.
- Writes: nothing, unless you ask for a copy on your computer.
- Costs: included in Pro and Agency, up to 25 reviews a day and 150 every 30 days for each workspace.

The review promises no views or sales. It tells you what to fix before you post.
